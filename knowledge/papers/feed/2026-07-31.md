## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **Optical Appearance of a Rotating Black Hole in Nonlinear Electrodynamics
  Surrounded by Thin Accretion Disks** — Abdul Malik Sultan, Manahil Ali,
  Muhammad Israr Aslam et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*. Backward ray tracing with
  a fisheye camera model under two illumination setups (celestial sphere and
  thin disk); tracks how the NED parameter and electric charge move the horizon,
  shadow, and photon ring for prograde vs retrograde flows. Methodologically the
  closest item this cycle to what the renderer actually does.
  *tier: numerical_approximation / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23093
- **Black hole shadow parameters and quasi-normal modes for Weyl-incorporated
  gravity** — Noraiz Tahir, Mubasher Jamil, Kaynat Fatima et al., 2026, *arXiv
  preprint*. Shadow observables and QNM frequencies for a static spherically
  symmetric metric in the modified relativistic dynamics (MORD) framework, where
  a matter–Weyl coupling term is added to the Einstein-Hilbert Lagrangian.
  *tier: speculative_extension / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23552
- **Constrained thermodynamics and geodesic observables of an effective
  non-commutative Kerr-like black hole** — H. Hassanabadi, L. A. López,
  N. Bretón et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*. Non-commutative deformation enters as
  a mass-dependent charge-like term but leaves the geodesic equations separable,
  so horizon structure, ergoregion, and stationary-limit surfaces shift while the
  Carter-constant machinery still applies. *tier: speculative_extension /
  preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27707

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Parametrized beyond-Teukolsky framework in the time domain** — Ciro De
  Simone, Sebastian H. Völkel, Kostas D. Kokkotas et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*.
  First time-domain implementation of the parametrized beyond-Teukolsky
  formalism, using (2+1)-D Gaussian-wave-packet scattering rather than solving
  the eigenvalue problem — gives access to amplitudes, phases, and late-time
  tails, not just QNM frequencies.
  *tier: numerical_approximation / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25311
- **Causal Green function decomposition for quantum black hole seismology** —
  Xi-Li Zhang, Jing Ren, 2026, *arXiv preprint*. Addresses the tension between
  QNM spectral instability under near-horizon modifications and the causality
  requirement that the prompt ringdown stay Kerr-like until the reflected signal
  returns; decomposes the Green function to relate time-domain waveform to the
  shifted spectrum. *tier: numerical_approximation / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26010
- **Echoes and quasinormal modes for static loop quantum black bounces** —
  Huajie Gong, Guoyang Fu, Shulan Li et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*. Finite-
  difference scalar perturbations of the loop-quantum black-bounce spacetime:
  single-barrier regular-black-hole configurations show no echoes, while the
  wormhole branch produces clear ones — an echo/no-echo discriminator tied to the
  effective-potential topology. *tier: speculative_extension / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25738

## Quantum gravity / information paradox / holography  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **Gravitational wave constraints on corrections to the Bekenstein-Hawking Area
  Formula in classical F(R) gravity and quantum GR** — Parthasarathi Majumdar,
  2026, *arXiv preprint*. Uses LVK binary-black-hole data — which confirms the
  Hawking area theorem at ~5σ for the loudest event — to bound corrections to the
  area–entropy relation, turning an entropy-side quantum-gravity question into an
  observational constraint. *tier: observational_constraint / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25513
- **Wald-like entropy and Islands in Dimensionally Reduced Einstein-Hilbert
  Gravity** — Krishna Jalan, 2026, *arXiv preprint*. Island formula and Page
  curve for eternal and quasi-stationary evaporating black holes in the 2D
  dilaton-gravity reduction of 4D Einstein-Hilbert, with Polyakov-Liouville
  quantum corrections. *tier: speculative_extension / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24883
- **Monodromy, Hidden Conformal Symmetry and Soft Hair in Kerr-MOG Black Hole** —
  Parthapratim Pradhan, 2026, *arXiv preprint*. Derives the 2D CFT dual of the
  Kerr-MOG geometry three independent ways (thermodynamics, monodromy, soft
  hair), finding consistent left/right temperatures and central charge but a
  non-universal, non-quantized entropy product.
  *tier: speculative_extension / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25205
