## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **Testing loop quantum gravity through EHT observations of M87* and Sgr A*
  using rotating holonomy-corrected black holes** — H. Ali et al., 2026. LQG
  holonomy correction parameter *b* enlarges the shadow; Kumar–Ghosh shadow-area +
  oblateness jointly fix spin *a* and *b*; current EHT angular-diameter bounds stay
  consistent with nonzero *b* (RHCBH viable vs. Kerr). *tier:
  observational_constraint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/831f23b668915ba6a70bd561b592d285/
- **Measuring the Black Hole and Accretion Parameters of Sagittarius A* from EHT
  Observations using a Semi-Analytic Model** — B. J. Marazzo-Nowicki et al., 2026.
  Bayesian hierarchical fit of EHT "snapshots" separating persistent strong-gravity
  structure from Sgr A* variability; 2017 data poorly constrain spin/field but favor
  near-face-on inclination (~9°) and near-horizon emission peak. *tier:
  observational_constraint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/678cadd2ae465278883f12f018437a8c/
- **Circular polarization images of Sgr A* for different magnetic field
  geometries** — H. Yin et al., 2026. Polarized radiative transfer over a RIAF in
  Kerr spacetime; CP dominated by Faraday conversion for radial/parabolic fields vs.
  intrinsic emission for dipole/vertical; ALMA CP excludes reversed-field model at
  high inclination. *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/ce603b1f067d537ea2e5960715186abc/

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Testing general relativity with binary black holes: sensitivity requirements
  for future space-based detectors** — T. Zhan et al., 2026. Quantifies the noise
  improvements TianQin/LISA/µAres would need to detect a nonlinear ringdown mode,
  displacement memory, and a putative beyond-GR signal from massive BBH mergers
  (4–9 orders of magnitude in the most demanding cases). *tier:
  numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/06bfe291c0dc5814903420345b1734ba/

## Quantum gravity / information paradox / holography  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **Entanglement islands, Page curves and phase transitions of Kerr-AdS black
  holes** — D. Das et al., 2026, *Eur. Phys. J. C*. Island rule applied to a
  rotating AdS black hole reproduces the unitary Page curve; a first-order phase
  transition induces a sharp discontinuity in the curve across ensembles. *tier:
  speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/763d3eb481ec5f6a9619313f9de3d6f2/
- **Holographic affinity: Weak gravity conjecture favors CFT thermodynamics in
  RN-AdS black holes with perfect fluid dark matter** — H. Van Quyet, 2026, *Annals
  of Physics*. Compares restricted-phase-space vs. CFT thermodynamics for RN-AdS +
  perfect-fluid dark matter; argues the holographic boundary description encodes
  Swampland (WGC/WCCC) consistency more efficiently than the bulk. *tier:
  speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/d2449cc8c1a959d998f3e927669fa2fb/
