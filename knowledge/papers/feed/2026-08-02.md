## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **Using horizon shadows to distinguish a black hole and a white hole** —
  Chengyu Bi, Zhoujian Cao, 2026, *arXiv preprint*. GR ray-tracing of accretion-
  disk images in the Kerr black-hole phase versus a post-bounce Kerr white-hole
  phase: the black hole gives the familiar crescent + central shadow, while the
  white hole produces eccentric nested intensity rings, plus polarized ray
  tracing showing an inter-ring polarization discontinuity as a proposed VLBI
  discriminator. Directly comparable to what the renderer computes.
  *tier: numerical_approximation / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20945
- **Quadrupolar tidal effects destroy the integrability of black hole geodesics:
  analytic proof and numerical evidence of chaos** — Paul Ramond, 2026, *arXiv
  preprint*. Analytic proof that a tidally induced quadrupole admits no conserved
  deformation of the Carter constant for generic tidal coupling and Kerr spin, so
  leading-order tidal dynamics is generically non-integrable; backed by Poincaré
  sections, Lyapunov exponents, and fractal escape-time basins. Relevant to any
  integrator that leans on Carter-constant separability.
  *tier: numerical_approximation / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27129
- **Extracting Energy from a Non-Kerr Rotating Spacetime with an Anomalous
  Quadrupole Moment via Magnetic Reconnection** — Ke Wang, Xiao-Xiong Zeng, 2026,
  *arXiv preprint*. Magnetic-reconnection energy extraction in a rotating
  spacetime with an anomalous quadrupole moment; closed timelike curve regions
  must be excised, and a small positive quadrupole gives the highest power and
  efficiency. *tier: speculative_extension / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25315

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Towards long and accurate numerical relativity waveforms of binary black
  holes beyond general relativity** — Guillermo Lara, Harald P. Pfeiffer,
  Nils Deppe et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*. Spectral methods plus the
  "fixing-the-equations" approach yield the longest genuine beyond-GR waveforms
  to date — shift-symmetric scalar-Gauss-Bonnet, equal-mass nonspinning,
  ≲1 rad phase error over 40+ GW cycles — with a distinguishable phase correction
  and earlier coalescence than GR. *tier: numerical_approximation / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27991
- **Dendro-GR at high mass ratios with high spins** — William K. Black,
  David W. Neilsen, Eric W. Hirschmann et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*. First
  Dendro-GR binaries at q=24 (nonspinning) and q=12 with χ up to 0.8, waveforms
  through ℓ=8, with stable constraint violations and low wall-hour cost —
  a proof of concept for the undersampled high-mass-ratio/high-spin corner.
  *tier: numerical_approximation / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26359
- **Fast, accurate, and differentiable: a neural-network surrogate for NRSur7dq4
  precessing binary black hole waveforms** — Michael Pürrer, Ashwin Girish,
  Lucy M. Thomas et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*. Per-quantity MLP surrogate for
  NRSur7dq4 validated on 10,000 waveforms (median mismatches 8e-5 to 1.7e-4);
  ~1 ms per waveform on an L40S and a fully differentiable JAX likelihood
  pipeline for gradient-based inference.
  *tier: numerical_approximation / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24960
- **Nonlinear Dynamics near the Threshold of Gravitational Collapse** —
  Jaime Redondo-Yuste, Josu C. Aurrekoetxea, 2026, *arXiv preprint*. NR
  simulations of self-gravitating scalar collapse used to bound where a
  flat-space perturbative expansion (to NNLO) breaks down: deviations appear
  before horizon formation once L_peak ~ 1e-2 L_Planck, with a redshifted driving
  frequency and power-law spectrum consistent with approximate discrete
  self-similarity. *tier: numerical_approximation / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27343

  preprint*. Argues for a Page-like transition in radiation *complexity* (not
  just entropy), read as a spectrum-dominated → basis-dominated crossover, and

  volume dominates — resolving a complexity analogue of the information paradox.
  *tier: speculative_extension / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21734
- **Holography in the linearized quantum gravity regime and modular crossed
  product** — Avinandan Mondal, 2026, *arXiv preprint*. Derives the JLMS relative-
  entropy equality for ball-shaped boundary regions from an isometric GNS
  embedding of linearized bulk gravity, then uses the modular crossed product to
  show the state-dependent entropy in the dressed Type-II algebra satisfies the
  vacuum-subtracted HRT formula. *tier: speculative_extension / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27337

  *arXiv preprint*. Credible-but-fringe: spacetime as an emergent fractal metric

  the macroscopic limit. Flagged for the speculative shelf only.
  *tier: speculative_extension / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26035
