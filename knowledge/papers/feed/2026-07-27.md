## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **From Morphology to Variability: Radiative Cooling Effects on Horizon-Scale
  Polarization in Two-Temperature GRMHD Simulations** — J. S. Long et al., 2026,
  *MNRAS*. Radiative cooling raises the effective Faraday depth and increases
  intrinsic ring/polarization asymmetry (extra power in non-quadrupolar βm modes),
  driving frequent sign reversals of ∠β2; 230 GHz polarization structure is largely
  insensitive to the electron distribution function. *tier:
  numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/c9a7f5eb333758399cbe47a040623a1f/
- **Dynamics and Radiative Signatures of Accretion Flows onto a Kerr-like
  Wormhole** — J.-Z. Xia et al., 2026, *ApJ*. GRMHD + GRRT (RAPTOR) of a magnetized
  torus around a Kerr black-bounce throat (ℓ = 2.5 M); throat-adjacent emission can
  dominate the 230 GHz image and imprints quasiperiodic light-curve modulation
  absent for Kerr. *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/947ac51844f356cf9e167ce634e6b13a/

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Black-hole–neutron-star mergers: New numerical-relativity simulations and
  multipolar effective-one-body model with spin precession and eccentricity** —
  2026, *Phys. Rev. D*. 52 new BHNS NR simulations in the tidal-disruption region
  with a full error budget; informs a multipolar EOB model (NQC + ringdown, remnant
  and recoil fits), reaching ~1% mismatches at low inclination. Data released via
  CoRe. *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/152161cf0dfa5ffc84c894ab594a88ee/
- **Fast gravitational waveform models for quasi-circular coalescences of neutron
  star–black hole binaries** — F. R. Vidal et al., 2026. First frequency-domain
  NSBH models with higher-order modes (IMRPhenomXHM_NSBH,
  SEOBNRv5HM_ROM_NRTidalv3_NSBH) plus a precessing extension; NR-calibrated tidal
  amplitude, validated against GWTC-3/4 events. *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/11312da7a59d55df8d40356dc6feb785/
- **Eccentric and unbound compact binaries in the LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA catalog:
  parameter estimation and waveform systematics with SEOBNRv6EHM** — L. Pompili
  et al., 2026. Shows eccentricity/mass/spin biases in earlier eccentric models;
  reanalyses 26 O1–O4 events, five with mild eccentricity support; direct-capture
  configurations for GW190521 are comparable but astrophysically unrealistic, so
  no unbound-origin claim. *tier: observational_constraint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/b2f192330f4a5d4b8c23d973155b0974/
- **Waveform stability of black hole ringdown with stochastic horizon structure**
  — H.-W. Hu et al., 2026, *Phys. Rev. D*. QNM spectral instability need not imply
  waveform breakdown: phase averaging over the wave equation suppresses sub-
  wavelength horizon detail (mismatch ∝ ε²), so incoherent quantum foam is
  unobservable and any real ringdown deviation implies macroscopically coherent
  horizon structure. *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/1c85fa9fea465836af8ed5c3a97b99af/

## Quantum gravity / information paradox / holography  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **Are Black Holes Fuzzballs? Probing Horizon-Scale Structure with LISA** —
  P. F. Muguruza et al., 2026. Forecasts LISA EMRI constraints on generic multipolar
  deviations from Kerr: non-axisymmetric mass quadrupole to ~10⁻³, axisymmetric mass
  octupole to ~10⁻², orders of magnitude beyond current bounds. *tier:
  speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/8a7e74cb660b511eb21160087234e83e/
- **Microstate Counting for rotating (type II) isolated horizons** — P. Nanda,
  2026, *preprint*. LQG entropy derivation extended to rotating horizons by slicing
  S² into concentric locally-nonrotating rings with constant effective Chern–Simons
  level, restoring a local CS description and including angular momentum. *tier:
  speculative_extension / preprint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/7c685175fdfa5a54a90ddbf0c14d12f6/
