## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **Qualitative Shadow Anomaly as a Smoking-gun Signature of Nonmetricity** —
  Zi-Chao Lin et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*. In nonmetricity gravity the photon
  trajectories widen rather than contract as the black-hole charge grows,
  inverting the usual charge–shadow trend and giving a qualitative (not merely
  quantitative) discriminator for next-generation horizon-scale imaging.
  *tier: speculative_extension / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23022
- **Viscosity effects on the shadow of a non-rotating black hole** —
  Sayantani Lahiri et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*. Adds shear viscosity to the
  accreting-fluid model and ray-traces at 230 GHz; the shadow edge is essentially
  unmoved while the surrounding emission is redistributed locally — a
  radiative-transfer-side effect rather than a geometry-side one.
  *tier: numerical_approximation / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21713
- **Plasma-Induced Modifications of the Shadows of Rotating Bardeen Black Holes
  with Perfect Fluid Dark Matter** — Gowtham Sidharth M et al., 2026, *arXiv
  preprint*. Null geodesics in a dispersive plasma around a regular rotating
  Bardeen metric embedded in a perfect-fluid dark-matter halo; plasma frequency
  and halo density both shift the apparent shadow size, so the two are partially
  degenerate against EHT diameter measurements.
  *tier: numerical_approximation / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19152
- **Dynamics and geometry of the inner sub-parsec-scale jet in 3C 279 observed
  with the Event Horizon Telescope** — Hendrik Müller et al., 2026, *arXiv
  preprint*. Multi-epoch EHT imaging of the 3C 279 core: compact core morphology
  plus apparent superluminal component motion, interpreted as jet bending toward
  the line of sight on sub-parsec scales. Real-data constraint on the emission
  geometry feeding horizon-scale models.
  *tier: observational_constraint / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16104

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **GWTC-5.0: Tests of General Relativity** — LIGO Scientific / Virgo / KAGRA
  Collaborations, 2026, *arXiv preprint*. Catalogue-level GR tests across 168
  events, including ringdown-consistency and inspiral–merger–ringdown checks; no
  evidence for deviations from GR is reported. This is the current
  observational ceiling any beyond-Kerr claim has to clear.
  *tier: observational_constraint / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19293
- **Constraining deviations from the Teukolsky equation with GW250114** —
  Sebastian H. Völkel, Nicola Franchini et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*. Bounds
  theory-agnostic modifications of the perturbation equation itself (rather than
  of individual QNM frequencies) using the GW250114 ringdown.
  *tier: observational_constraint / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26561
- **Resumming Kerr Quasinormal-Mode Frequencies: Accuracy and Breakdown Near
  Extremality** — Jierui Hu, Kent Yagi, Nicolás Yunes et al., 2026, *arXiv
  preprint*. Analytic resummed approximations to Kerr QNM frequencies, with an
  explicit characterisation of where the expansion fails as a → M — relevant if
  the renderer ever needs cheap QNM frequencies instead of tabulated ones.
  *tier: numerical_approximation / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27043
- **Discrete symmetries of modified Teukolsky equations** — Ciro De Simone
  et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*. Shows how modifications to the perturbation
  potential break or preserve the discrete (isospectrality-type) symmetries of
  the Teukolsky system, and how that propagates into the QNM spectrum.
  *tier: numerical_approximation / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27053
- **Schrödinger perturbation theory for black hole quasinormal modes** —
  Jacopo Lestingi, Laura Sberna, Stephen R. Green et al., 2026, *arXiv
  preprint*. Builds a bilinear-form framework giving QNM frequency shifts to
  arbitrary perturbative order, in direct analogy with quantum-mechanical
  perturbation theory. *tier: numerical_approximation / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19492

## Quantum gravity / information paradox / holography  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **The black hole S-matrix in gauge/gravity duality** — Emil J. Martinec, 2026,
  *arXiv preprint*. Constructs an S-matrix for brane-shell formation and decay
  in the holographic dual, offering an information-paradox resolution in which
  the "black hole" is a long-lived resonance rather than a geometry with a
  horizon interior. *tier: speculative_extension / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18393
- **Universality of Magic in Local Quantum Field Theory** — Valentin Benedetti
  et al., 2026, *arXiv preprint*. Argues stabilizer (zero-magic) states cannot
  reproduce QFT vacuum entanglement structure, which bounds how far
  quantum-error-correction toy models of holography can be pushed as literal
  descriptions. *tier: speculative_extension / preprint.*
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16403
