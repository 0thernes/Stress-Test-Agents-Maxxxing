## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **Prospects for Improving the Theoretical Uncertainty for Tests of General
  Relativity with the EHT** — L. Medeiros et al., 2026. Calibrates the bright-ring
  vs. analytic-shadow size relation across a large GRMHD image library; 345 GHz
  observing, PRIMO ring-width priors, jet-aligned spin axis and MAD assumptions
  each shrink the theoretical error budget. *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/1f2d41342969592fa4de171754bde239/
- **Probing Kalb–Ramond gravity with charged rotating black holes: constraints
  from EHT observations** — T. A. Nengroo et al., 2026, *Physics of the Dark
  Universe*. Lorentz-violating parameter ℓ suppresses shadow radius by √(1−ℓ);
  Sgr A* angular diameter gives ℓ ≲ 0.19. *tier: observational_constraint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/17a489dbb28f5fcab5bee98fcb642f03/
- **Rotating Black Holes with Primary Scalar Hair: Shadow Signatures in Beyond
  Horndeski Gravity** — K. Nozari et al., 2026. Primary scalar hair Q as a third
  hair parameter; M87* θ_d = 42 ± 3 μas and ΔC ≤ 0.1 restrict but do not exclude
  Q > 0; deviations are O(μas), near current instrument threshold. *tier:
  observational_constraint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/59c3678fb6f4547a818f28e7bdb86684/
- **Shadows and lensing signatures of a rotating black hole in a Hernquist dark
  matter halo** — A. A. A. Filho et al., 2026. Newman–Janis-generated Kerr-like
  halo metric; halo enlarges the photon capture region; Sgr A* bounds
  ρ̂ ~ (2.7–3.8)×10⁻³ at 1σ, plus strong/weak lensing observables. *tier:
  numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/26cb514b843e524e9f8a1e319fcf231a/

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Black hole spectroscopy: from theory to experiment** — E. Berti et al., 2026,
  *Classical and Quantum Gravity*. State-of-the-art review of QNM prediction,
  excitation and ringdown modeling in GR and modified gravity, plus LVK data
  analysis status and LISA/next-gen prospects. *tier: review /
  numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/b83338f1ecc55bf2b6d19437722ddfac/
- **A nonlinear voice from GW250114 ringdown** — Y.-F. Wang et al., 2026. Claims
  first observational evidence of quadratic QNMs (six nonlinear modes from
  (2,2,0) and its first two overtones), Bayes factor 74, zero amplitude excluded
  at 3.0σ. *tier: preprint / observational_constraint (contested).*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/4fea862c7abd56cdb8f89fa8c9647d40/
- **Modeling Direct Waves in Binary Black Hole Ringdowns** — R. Dyer et al., 2026.
  Extracts the non-QNM "direct wave" from NR waveforms; its frequency deviates
  from the horizon value, weakening its use as a horizon probe. *tier:
  numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/6085f1e39c8651f1aae6be6c1cfed84d/
- **Highly eccentric non-spinning binary black hole mergers: quadrupolar
  post-merger waveforms** — N. Rao et al., 2026. Closed-form (2,2) post-merger
  model fit to 233 RIT eccentric simulations; mismatches ~10⁻³ up to near-extreme
  eccentricity. *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/259831acd2f95f7285685a75bf5a90df/

## Quantum gravity / information paradox  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **Seeing Page Curves and Islands with Blinders On** — H. Geng et al., 2026.
  Argues the bulk Hilbert space does not factorize radially, so information is
  already encoded in asymptotic observables; Page curves arise only after
  removing the Hamiltonian from the exterior algebra. *tier:
  speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/e0cf95ddee2855b3a7437c604f52ff1a/
- **Asymptotic Algebras and Holography of Information in CGHS Model** —
  W. A. Dar et al., 2026. Builds the radiative phase space at future null
  infinity for CGHS; argues island existence must violate commutativity of the
  left/right boundary algebras. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/eb03e698801d5195be1e17d03bdc67e2/
- **Microscopic Origin of Page Curve** — A. Averin, 2026. Generalized
  Ryu–Takayanagi tied to Hamiltonian surface charges on the bifurcation surface
  as the microstates carrying Wald entropy. *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/22dda35fc5b6528f9deb55b8ecb9e180/
