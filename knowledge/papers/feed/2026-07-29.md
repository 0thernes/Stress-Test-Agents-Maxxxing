## Black-hole shadows / Kerr spin / EHT  *(directly validates the renderer)*

- **The Black Hole Explorer: A Space VLBI Mission Concept** — D. Marrone, 2026,
  *USNC-URSI National Radio Science Meeting*. Mission-design overview of BHEX, a
  NASA SMEX concept flying a 3.5 m antenna at 100 and 300 GHz from ~20,000 km
  altitude, reaching ~6 µas resolution in concert with ground stations — roughly
  3× finer than current EHT imaging. *tier: observational_constraint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/195517fb10825a82a98dff2cdcd569db/
- **Dark matter halo inference with higher order photon rings and closure data in
  multi frequency VLBI** — A. Pradhan et al., 2026, *Nuclear Physics B*. Closure-
  domain forecasting framework: a perturbative spherical halo (NFW/Dehnen/
  Hernquist) coherently deforms the near-critical ring geometry, producing
  correlated order-dependent shifts across the subring hierarchy; quantifies when
  long-baseline closure phases/amplitudes enter the photon-ring regime for M87*
  and Sgr A*. *tier: numerical_approximation.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/71fc65b05e7f5a368b443727454ed249/
- **Probing Rotating Einstein-Power-Yang-Mills Black Holes through Shadows and
  Quasinormal Modes: Prospects for Event Horizon Telescope Constraints** —
  I. Ezzaki et al., 2026. Newman–Janis-generated Kerr-like metric with spin a,
  Yang–Mills charge Q and power q; EHT angular size + Schwarzschild deviation
  bound Q ≲ 0.26 M at 1σ at the Maxwell point q = 1, with the bound weakening as
  q → 3/2 where shadow diameter becomes charge-insensitive. WKB scalar QNM shifts
  sit below current detector resolution. *tier: observational_constraint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/a180aa90588f5c75b44f9f5c86192e0e/

## Numerical relativity / ringdown / QNM  *(adjacent observational GR)*

- **Deterministic Trust Regions for Finite-Window Black-Hole Spectroscopy in
  GW250114** — Ruiliang Li, 2026, *preprint*. Proves a deterministic frequency-
  extraction bound for a projected Prony/matrix-pencil pipeline and builds a local
  inverse atlas for the Kerr (2,2,0) map, propagating error into (2,2,1) and
  (4,4,0) consistency tests. Accepted windows form an intermediate post-peak band —
  earlier windows are start-time-drift sensitive, later ones variance-dominated.
  *tier: observational_constraint / preprint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/2e41fb4d7686530795e094b79d6fc27b/
- **A pre-merger-informed spectral-level ringdown inference framework for
  black-hole spectroscopy** — S. Guo et al., 2026, *preprint*. SPRING separates
  spectral measurement from theory-side (Kerr / beyond-Kerr) interpretation by
  propagating pre-merger amplitude-scale information into an agnostic two-component
  damped-sinusoid ringdown fit; on GW250114 this raises Bayesian support by
  Δln B ~ 5–10 while the remnant posterior stays consistent with the IMR estimate.
  *tier: observational_constraint / preprint.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/c3b23f7677f757b0b5ddc7fbc4925b28/

## Quantum gravity / information paradox / holography  *(speculative context — label, do not let it touch the kernel)*

- **Generalized Entropy is von Neumann Entropy II: The complete symmetry group and
  edge modes** — M. Klinger et al., 2026. Angle-dependent horizon-area
  fluctuations at the same perturbative order introduce horizon edge-mode charges
  tied to an infinite-dimensional "boost supertranslation" symmetry; the resulting
  constrained algebra is Type II with a universal trace. Black-hole exteriors give
  Type II∞ (no maximum-entropy state); the de Sitter static patch gives Type II₁
  once the observer's rotational and binding energy are modeled.
  *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/ad84ac6b0a8c5466aa5553c46697bc8b/
- **An Algebraic Resolution of the Firewall Paradox** — N. Kumar, 2026,
  *Int. J. Mod. Phys. D*. Argues gravitational dressing blocks the tensor-product
  factorization AMPS assumes: within a fixed asymptotic charge sector the interior
  partner algebra is contained in (not commuting with) the dressed radiation
  algebra, so entanglement monogamy never applies — unitarity and horizon
  smoothness coexist without islands or replica wormholes.
  *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/46bf2134b5e55a1d8911adf171838ddf/
- **"It from Bit": The Hartle-Hawking state and quantum mechanics for de Sitter
  observers** — Y. Zhao, 2026. Claims the one-state property of closed universes is
  compatible with finite-dimensional QM for an interior de Sitter observer, by
  distinguishing the (commutative, classical-probability) baby-universe Hilbert
  space from the bulk Hilbert space; demonstrated in a solvable 1D topological toy
  model, with de Sitter entropy read as coarse-grained entropy.
  *tier: speculative_extension.*
  https://consensus.app/papers/details/20ab5db2428c54cd81584948a2fa7306/
